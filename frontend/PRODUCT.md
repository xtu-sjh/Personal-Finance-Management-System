# Product

## Register

product

## Users

An individual managing their own personal finances. They open the app a few times per week to log transactions, check their budget status, and review spending patterns. Their context is casual: a phone or laptop at home, not a high-pressure work environment. The primary job is "know where my money goes and stay within budget."

## Product Purpose

A personal finance management system for tracking income, expenses, budgets, and spending categories. The app exists to give one person a clear, always-available view of their financial picture. Success looks like: the user opens the app, logs a transaction or checks a budget, and closes it in under two minutes. No friction, no confusion, no anxiety.

## Brand Personality

**Calm, clear, trustworthy.**

The interface should feel like a well-organized desk: everything has a place, nothing demands attention unnecessarily. The app doesn't gamify spending, doesn't send push notifications to drive engagement, doesn't use urgency or color to create anxiety. It presents data honestly and lets the user decide what to do with it.

Voice: neutral, informative, concise. Labels are direct ("Income", "Expense", "Budget"). No motivational copy, no "Great job!" messages, no emoji in UI text.

## Anti-references

**Corporate banking software.** No cold gray slabs, no dense multi-column forms, no "enterprise software" energy. The app should not feel like filling out a tax form or navigating a bank's online portal. Avoid: tiny text, cramped spacing, jargon-heavy labels, gray-on-gray color schemes, institutional tone.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration.** Every visual element earns its place through function. Color signals meaning (green = income, red = expense), not decoration. Spacing creates rhythm, not filler.

2. **The tool disappears into the task.** The user should never think about the interface. They think about their money. Fast interactions, predictable layouts, familiar patterns.

3. **Data speaks first.** Numbers, charts, and tables are the primary content. The UI frame (navigation, headers, cards) stays minimal so the financial data gets maximum attention.

4. **Trust through consistency.** Same button styles, same form patterns, same color meanings across every screen. Consistency builds trust; inconsistency raises doubt.

5. **No financial anxiety triggers.** No flashing red numbers, no countdown timers, no "you're over budget!" alerts with alarm icons. Budget warnings use calm amber, not urgent red. The app informs, it doesn't judge.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. The app is in Chinese (zh-CN); ensure CJK text renders well at all sizes. Consider that financial data is often read quickly: high contrast for numbers, adequate font sizes for amounts. No motion that conveys critical information (all motion is decorative/state-feedback only).
